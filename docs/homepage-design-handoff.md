# Product homepage implementation — 2026-09-11

Design reference: [Figma desktop 1440px](https://www.figma.com/design/NqkKDv45BF9Jcyc5Mec4tP?node-id=5-537), [mobile 390px](https://www.figma.com/design/NqkKDv45BF9Jcyc5Mec4tP?node-id=15-15).

The homepage now presents custom outlines, interlock templates, generation, Apple devices, and actual artwork. At the user's request, the SVG editing feature block and its interface-guide preview are removed from the homepage. Tutorials remain on `/learn` and `/help`. Homepage FAQ markup and its FAQPage structured data are removed together. The header links to Features, Showcase, Pricing, and Help; homepage downloads appear only in the hero and closing section. Repeated header and devices-section prompts are removed. All remaining download CTAs reuse the existing, unmodified Apple App Store badge, with localized accessible labels.
